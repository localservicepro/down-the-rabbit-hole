## How Often Should You Mow Your Lawn in Canberra?
| Season | Recommended Mowing Frequency | Key Considerations |
|---|
| Spring | Every 1–2 weeks | Rapid growth phase requires frequent attention. |
| Summer | Every 2–3 weeks | Avoid cutting too short to prevent sun stress and water loss. |
| Autumn | Every 3–4 weeks | Clear fallen leaves and prepare the lawn for dormancy. |
| Winter | Every 4–6 weeks (or as needed) | Minimal growth; mow only if necessary on a high setting. |

Lawn mowing frequency in Canberra depends heavily on the season; lawns typically need mowing every 1-2 weeks in the fast-growing spring and summer, and less often, around every 3-4 weeks, in autumn and winter. Adjusting your mowing schedule to the rate of growth is crucial for lawn health.

Over-mowing can stress the grass, while under-mowing can lead to a messy, overgrown yard that becomes difficult to manage. A professional service will automatically adjust the schedule based on the weather and seasonal growth patterns.

### Do I need to be home for the lawn mowing service?

Most lawn care companies do not require you to be home for the service, as long as they have safe access to the areas that need mowing. It's always best to confirm access arrangements beforehand, especially for properties with pets or locked gates.
### What's included in a standard lawn mowing service?

A standard service typically includes mowing the entire lawn, trimming the edges for a neat finish, and blowing grass clippings off paths and driveways. Some companies may also include clipping removal or offer it for an additional fee.
### Why is professional edging important?

Professional edging creates a clean, sharp line between your lawn and other surfaces like sidewalks, driveways, and garden beds. This detail significantly enhances curb appeal and gives your property a professionally manicured look that mowing alone cannot achieve.
### Can a lawn be mowed when it's wet?

Mowing wet grass is generally not recommended. It can result in an uneven cut, clog the mower, and leave clumps of wet clippings on the lawn which can smother the grass underneath. Professional services will often reschedule if the lawn is too wet to ensure a quality result.
