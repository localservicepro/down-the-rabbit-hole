## What Are DVA Lawn Care Services?

DVA lawn care services are government-funded maintenance programs for eligible Australian veterans. The primary goal is to ensure a veteran’s home and garden are safe, accessible, and well-maintained, reducing the risks of falls or injury and removing the physical burden of yard work.

These services are a form of home maintenance assistance; DVA can tell you which of its programs applies to you. They are not for cosmetic enhancements but for essential upkeep. This includes tasks like regular mowing, trimming overgrown shrubs, and clearing pathways of green waste. The focus is always on function and safety.
### Who is the Program For?

This program is specifically for veterans who have an assessed need for assistance. The Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA) determines eligibility, and your card type (Gold or White) can affect what is available, so confirm your entitlement with DVA. It acknowledges that service-related injuries or health conditions can make strenuous activities like lawn mowing difficult or dangerous.

### What is a DVA White Card vs. a Gold Card?

A DVA Gold Card entitles the holder to treatment for all medical conditions, regardless of whether they are service-related. A DVA White Card provides coverage for specific, DVA-accepted service-related injuries or conditions. DVA decides whether your card and needs make you eligible for garden help, so confirm with DVA.
