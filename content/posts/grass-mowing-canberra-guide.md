Canberra's distinct four seasons, with hot, dry summers and cold, frosty winters, demand a specific and adaptable approach to lawn care. Unlike more temperate climates, our lawns face significant environmental stress, from scorching sun to deep frosts, which directly impacts their health and mowing needs.

The soil around Canberra can often be heavy and clay-based, which affects drainage and nutrient absorption. Furthermore, the common grass types that thrive here—cool-season varieties like Tall Fescue and warm-season grasses like Kikuyu—have different growth cycles and requirements. Understanding these local factors is the first step toward effective lawn maintenance.
## How often should I mow my lawn in Canberra?

Mowing frequency in Canberra depends entirely on the season and your grass type. As a general rule, you should mow weekly in the peak growing seasons of spring and autumn, every 7-10 days in summer, and reduce to every 3-4 weeks during the winter dormancy.
### Spring Mowing Tips

As temperatures rise, your lawn wakes up and begins to grow rapidly. Start by setting your mower blades higher for the first mow of the season. Gradually lower them to the ideal height over the next few weeks. Mowing weekly is usually necessary to keep up with the vigorous growth.
### Summer Mowing Strategy

Canberra summers can be harsh. To help your lawn cope, raise the mowing height to provide more shade for the soil, reducing water evaporation and protecting the roots. This also helps the grass develop deeper, more drought-resistant roots. Stick to a 7-10 day schedule, but avoid mowing during the hottest part of the day to reduce stress on both you and the grass.
### Autumn and Winter Care

In autumn, your lawn is recovering and preparing for winter. Maintain a regular mowing schedule as growth slows. For the final mow before winter sets in, lower the blade height slightly to allow more sunlight to reach the base of the grass, which can help prevent winter fungal diseases. During winter, mowing is infrequent and should only be done if necessary on dry, frost-free days.
## The 'One-Third' Rule: Mowing for Health

The single most important principle of healthy mowing is the 'one-third' rule. This states that you should never remove more than one-third of the grass blade's height in a single mowing session. Cutting too much at once shocks the plant, depletes its energy reserves, and exposes it to disease and pests.

Following this rule encourages strong root development, which is essential for a resilient lawn. For example, if you want to maintain a lawn height of 4cm, you should mow it when it reaches 6cm. ### Should I water my lawn before or after mowing?

It is best to water your lawn a day or two before mowing, not immediately before or after. Mowing wet grass is difficult and can damage the lawn and mower. Watering right after mowing can also stress the freshly cut grass blades.
### What is lawn aeration and is it necessary in Canberra?

Lawn aeration involves creating small holes in the soil to reduce compaction and allow air, water, and nutrients to reach the roots. It's highly beneficial in Canberra, where soil can be heavy and compacted, especially after a hot, dry summer.
