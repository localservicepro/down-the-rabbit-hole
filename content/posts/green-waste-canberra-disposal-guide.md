## What exactly is green waste?

Green waste is any organic material generated from your garden or yard. This category includes everything from lawn clippings and fallen leaves to tree branches and pruned shrubs. Think of it as nature's leftovers from your outdoor space.

Common examples of green waste include:

Grass clippings

Leaves and flowers

Weeds (before they go to seed)

Twigs and small branches

Hedge trimmings

Palm fronds

It's just as important to know what isn't green waste. Items like soil, rocks, concrete, plastic pots, garden hoses, and treated timber should not be mixed in. These materials can contaminate the recycling process and require different disposal methods.
## Why is proper green waste disposal so important?

Properly disposing of your green waste is about more than just keeping your yard tidy. It has significant benefits for the environment, your property, and the wider Canberra community.
### Reduces Environmental Impact

When green waste ends up in a landfill, it decomposes without oxygen, releasing methane. This is a greenhouse gas far more potent than carbon dioxide. Organic waste is a major source of landfill emissions. By diverting green waste to composting facilities, we turn it into a valuable resource and significantly cut our environmental footprint.
### Keeps Your Property Safe and Healthy

Large, unmanaged piles of green waste can be more than just an eyesore. They can become a serious fire hazard, especially during Canberra's hot, dry summers. A cluttered yard can also attract pests like rodents and snakes, creating an unsafe environment for your family and pets. Maintaining a clean yard in suburbs like Kambah or Fisher contributes to overall community safety.

## What are my green waste disposal options in Canberra?

Canberra residents have several convenient options for managing green waste. The best choice depends on the volume of waste and how much time and effort you want to invest. Let's break them down.
### 1. ACT Government Green Waste Bin

For regular, small amounts of garden waste, the fortnightly green bin service is perfect. It’s an easy way to dispose of lawn clippings, weeds, and small prunings. The collected material is then professionally composted, contributing to the ACT's sustainability goals.
### 2. Drop-Off at Resource Management Centres

If you have more than a bin's worth, you can take it to a Resource Management Centre. The main locations are in Mugga Lane and Mitchell. This is a good DIY option if you have a trailer or ute, but be mindful that fees often apply based on the weight or volume of your load.
### 3. Professional Green Waste Removal Services

When dealing with a massive amount of waste from a big project, a professional service is the most efficient choice. The provider comes to your property, loads the waste, and hauls it away. This is ideal for post-storm cleanups, preparing a property for sale, or clearing a completely overgrown yard. Companies specializing in rubbish removal and yard cleanups have the right equipment to get the job done quickly and safely.

### What can I put in my Canberra green waste bin?

You can put most organic garden materials in your Canberra green bin. This includes lawn clippings, leaves, flowers, weeds, and small branches under 10cm thick and 1.5m long. Things like soil, rocks, plastic bags, and treated timber are not allowed. It's always best to check the ACT Government's guidelines if you're unsure.

### Is it better to compost or use a green bin?

Both are great environmental choices. Composting at home is fantastic for creating nutrient-rich soil for your own garden, but it requires space and effort. The council green bin service is a convenient alternative that also ensures the waste is professionally composted or mulched on a larger scale, contributing to a circular economy.
