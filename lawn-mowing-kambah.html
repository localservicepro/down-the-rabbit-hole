What we do on Kambah blocks

Mowing is the core, but on a 1970s Kambah block there is usually more to keep on top of. Our lawn mowing Kambah visits can include the following.

Residential and commercial properties are both welcome, including rentals and townhouse complexes.

  • Mowing front, back and nature strip, cut to suit the season
  • Edging along paths, driveways and garden beds
  • Hedge and shrub trimming to keep them off paths and windows
  • Yard clean-ups when a garden has got away from you
  • Green waste removal available every visit

Why regular mowing suits larger Tuggeranong blocks

A big block rewards routine. Kikuyu and couch lawns on Kambah's older blocks put on a lot of growth from September, and if the lawn gets away it takes several cuts to bring it back. Regular lawn mowing Kambah wide keeps grass at a steady height, which shades out bindi and broadleaf weeds and holds moisture through the hot, dry summer.

Many Kambah frontages are wide and sloped, and a fortnightly cut is far less work than a monthly rescue.

Kambah through the seasons

Kambah sits under Mount Taylor and beside the Urambi Hills, and the cold air settles here. Hard frosts from May to August stop most lawns growing, so we drop to a monthly tidy and keep the height up. From September the growth surge arrives and we move to fortnightly.

Autumn brings leaf drop from the deciduous street trees, and we rake, mow and remove it with the clippings. In summer we cut higher and less often, so the lawn holds moisture while water restrictions are a consideration.

Questions about lawn mowing in Kambah

How much does mowing cost in Kambah?

It depends on the size of the block, the state of the lawn and what else is included, so we quote each lawn mowing Kambah job individually. Quotes are free and upfront, and we will not guess a price without seeing the block.

Do you cover all of Kambah, including the nature strip?

Yes. Our lawn mowing Kambah service covers the whole suburb and its neighbours, Wanniassa, Fisher, Waramanga and Greenway. Nature strips can be included in the visit, and edged along the kerb and footpath so the frontage looks finished.

Do you take the grass clippings and green waste away?

Yes. Green waste removal is available with every lawn mowing Kambah visit, and with garden maintenance and clean-ups too. If you would rather keep some clippings for compost or mulch, tell us and we will leave what you want.

Can you take on a Kambah lawn that has not been cut all winter?

Yes. We do a first cut to bring it down in stages, tidy the edges and remove the waste, then set a regular schedule so it does not get away again. Overgrown yards are quoted after a look, because the first visit is more work.
